KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Common Online Scams

Scammers use different tricks to steal money, passwords and personal information.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails or messages that try to trick you into clicking dangerous links or sharing personal information.

⚠️ Check the sender and link before clicking.
💳

UPI Scams

Scammers may send fake payment requests or ask you to scan a QR code to steal money.

⚠️ You don't need a PIN to receive money.
🛒

Fake Shopping

Fake shopping websites may offer products at extremely low prices and steal your money.

⚠️ Check the website before making payment.
💼

Fake Job Offers

Fake recruiters may promise easy jobs and ask for registration fees or personal data.

⚠️ Never pay money to get a job.
🔢

OTP Scams

Scammers may pretend to be bank employees and ask for your OTP or verification code.

⚠️ Never share your OTP with anyone.
📱

Social Media Scams

Fake profiles and hacked accounts can be used to ask friends for money or information.

⚠️ Verify unusual requests before responding.
