SECURE YOUR ACCOUNTS

Password Safety

A strong password is one of the simplest ways to protect your online accounts.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords using a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters.

✔ Example: River@92Moon!
🔄

Don't Reuse Passwords

Avoid using the same password for multiple accounts. If one account is hacked, others could also be at risk.

✘ Same password everywhere
📲

Use Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account.

✔ Password + Verification Code
🚫

Never Share Passwords

Banks, trusted companies and genuine support teams will not ask you to share your password.

✘ Never send your password by message
🔑

Update Important Passwords

Change passwords when you suspect that your account may have been compromised.

✔ Act quickly after a security problem
🗝️

Use a Password Manager

A password manager can help you create and safely store unique passwords.

✔ One secure place for passwords
🛡️

Remember

Your password is private. Never share it with strangers, friends or anyone who asks unexpectedly.
