STAY SAFE ON SOCIAL MEDIA

Social Media Safety

Social media connects us with people around the world. But sharing too much information can put your privacy at risk.

👤

Watch for Fake Profiles

Scammers may create fake accounts using someone else's name, photos or identity.

  • Check the profile carefully.
  • Look for unusual activity.
  • Don't trust new accounts immediately.
🔒

Protect Your Privacy

Avoid publicly sharing information that scammers could use against you.

  • Don't share your address.
  • Avoid posting private documents.
  • Review your privacy settings.
🔗

Avoid Suspicious Links

Messages containing unexpected links can lead to fake websites or malware.

  • Don't click unknown links.
  • Check the website address.
  • Verify unexpected messages.
🎭

Beware of Impersonation

Someone may pretend to be your friend, family member or another trusted person.

  • Verify unusual requests.
  • Call the person directly.
  • Never send money immediately.
📶

Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Public networks may not always be secure, especially when accessing sensitive accounts.

  • Avoid sensitive transactions.
  • Use trusted networks when possible.
  • Log out after using shared devices.
🚨

Report Suspicious Accounts

If you find a suspicious profile or scam, report it instead of interacting with it.

  • Use the platform's report option.
  • Block suspicious accounts.
  • Tell someone you trust.

Think Before You Share.

Once information is posted online, it can be difficult to completely remove it.
